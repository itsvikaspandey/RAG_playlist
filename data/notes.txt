RFC-104: Enterprise Knowledge Ingestion & RAG Standards
Status: APPROVED | Version: 2.4 | Classification: INTERNAL ENGINEERING
Author: AI Infrastructure & Platform Architecture Group
Last Updated: Q3 2025

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OBJECTIVE
This document establishes mandatory data ingestion standards for all Retrieval-Augmented
Generation (RAG) pipelines deployed within enterprise knowledge bases. Adherence to these
guidelines ensures high vector search precision, eliminates chunk fragmentation, and
guarantees sub-100ms P99 retrieval latency.

2. DOCUMENT INGESTION SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS (SLAs)
- Ingestion Throughput: Minimum 250 pages per second across distributed worker nodes.
- Parsing Fidelity: 100% preservation of structured tabular data into Markdown representations.
- Metadata Completeness: All canonical Document objects must include verified source path,
  page index, department code, and deterministic content hashes.

3. SUPPORTED INGESTION CHANNELS & FORMATS
- Technical Architecture Docs (.md, .txt): Native UTF-8 parsing with header hierarchy preserved.
- Enterprise Financial Reports (.pdf): Table-aware bounding box extraction via PyMuPDF / Docling.
- Metric Telemetry & Spreadsheets (.csv): Row-level document serialization with primary keys.
- Corporate Handbooks & SOPs (.docx): Heading hierarchy and list extraction via Docx2txt.

4. COMPLIANCE & GOVERNANCE
All ingested content is subject to automated Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) filtering.
Confidential engineering memos must never be co-indexed with public-facing customer documentation.
